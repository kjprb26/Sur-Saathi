How to get clean results
  1. Use a vocal-only track if you can. Pitch tracking follows the loudest clear pitch. On a full mix, tabla, harmonium or strings can pull it off the singer. Run the song through a vocal separator first (Demucs, UVR, or any "vocal remover" site) and load the vocal stem. Then play the stem or the full song, whichever you prefer to hear.
  2. Wear headphones. If the song comes out of speakers, the mic hears it and your line gets mixed up with the singer's.
  3. Check Sa. It's detected from the most common note plus its Pa. If the gridlines look shifted, choose Sa by ear with the Tanpura button on.
  4. Slow down difficult taans to 0.6–0.75×. The pitch stays the same, and the notes are spread out so you can follow the meend and gamak.
  5. Riyaz a phrase: while playing, press Loop A at the start and Loop B at the end. Each pass scores you, and the last pass is shown under Accuracy.
  6. Shortcuts: Space play/pause, ←/→ 5 s, [/] loop A/B, M mic. Drag the main view to scrub; tap it to play or pause.
  7. Microphone needs HTTPS. Open this page from an https:// address (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel) or from localhost. Opened as a plain file, some browsers block the mic.
